Background

About me

I'm from Pakistan, and my working life has spanned business, banking, distribution and sales, and various trade activities.

I completed my early education, then finished my F.Sc. from 1985 to 1987, followed by a B.Sc. from Science College.

My professional life also includes private employment in Lahore and a period working at the National Bank of Pakistan.

After that, I entered the vehicle accessories (auto parts) business in Mandi Bahauddin, where I gained roughly 27 years of hands-on business experience — covering customer dealing, buying and selling, business management, understanding market needs, and distribution.

Today, my interests lie in artificial intelligence, digital content, social media, online education, YouTube, digital marketing, and new online business opportunities.
